---

## Platform Context

Your marketplace's core pitch to these sellers:
- **Upload products → get paid** — no complicated setup
- **Lower fees than Takealot** — Takealot charges 8–15%+ referral fees + fulfilment. Your platform can undercut this significantly for solo and small sellers
- **Built-in payment processing** — removes the friction of "WhatsApp me to pay via EFT" which kills conversions
- **Legitimate storefront** — many social sellers lose sales because buyers don't trust an Instagram DM checkout

## MARKET SIZE CONTEXT

- **SA social commerce users (2024):** ~12 million active social media users engage with businesses online
- **Facebook Marketplace SA:** Estimated 4–5 million monthly users
- **TikTok SA:** ~10.6 million users; TikTok Shop not yet officially launched in SA — **huge opportunity**
- **Instagram Shopping SA:** Growing but fragmented — no unified SA checkout solution for micro sellers
- **WhatsApp Business SA:** Millions of micro-sellers use WhatsApp catalogs as their only storefront; cannot process card payments through it
- **Takealot referral fees:** 8–15% + 3% fulfilment + R6/unit pick fee — prohibitive for low-margin small sellers
- **Gap your platform fills:** SA's micro-sellers have the audience but lack the payment infrastructure. You provide the missing checkout layer.

---

## PRIORITY SECTORS FOR FIRST 10 SELLERS

If onboarding is manual/limited, target these sectors first for the fastest traction:

1. **Home food sellers** — highest WhatsApp resale activity; biggest pain with EFT payments
2. **Fashion/clothing** — highest TikTok/Instagram volume; biggest "checkout friction" problem
3. **Natural beauty/hair** — fastest growing, repeat-purchase category
4. **Handmade crafts** — currently selling via Etsy (USD fees) or Instagram DM; SA ZAR store is a major unlock
5. **BobShop sellers (small ones)** — already proven but paying marketplace fees; easiest to convert
