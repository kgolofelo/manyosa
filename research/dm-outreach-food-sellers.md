## DMing tips

- **Send between 5pm–9pm** — that's when SA TikTok engagement peaks.
- **Like 2–3 of their recent videos first** so your DM doesn't look cold.
- **Don't send the same opener to multiple accounts in one hour** — TikTok throttles repeated DMs.
- **If they have a WhatsApp number listed, use WhatsApp instead** — much higher reply rate. Open with: *"Hi, found you on TikTok 👋..."*
- **Track replies in a simple spreadsheet:** handle | sent date | response | next step.
