### Strategic read-out
- **Cape Town and Joburg/Sandton dominate**; Pretoria, Durban, Stellenbosch are under-served boutique territories.
- **Crowded segment:** generalist mobile + web mid-market dev (50+ companies).
- **Less crowded / opportunity gaps:**
  - AI-augmented internal-tools / workflow automation for SA SMEs (only SIVOXI, Warp explicitly lead with this).
  - Vertical specialists: SA agritech, mining-ops, insure-tech middleware, township-economy commerce — almost no specialised shops.
  - Pricing transparency / productised offers (a few like Scrums.com and Cleveroad publish fixed packages; most don't).
  - Afrikaans-first / regional language tooling and POPIA-native compliance products.
- **Differentiation levers that work in ZA:** local POPIA fluency, ZAR-denominated fixed-price packages, founder-led delivery (clients are wary of "salesperson hand-off"), and case studies in their specific sector.

## PART 2 — METHODOLOGY: HOW TO IDENTIFY SA COMPANIES "ACTIVELY SEEKING DEV HELP"

The list in Part 3 is curated, but to keep your pipeline alive use these recurring signals (in priority order):

1. **Job board "active hiring" signal** (strongest intent):
   - OfferZen "Hire developers" client list (companies posting Senior/Lead engineer roles → often understaffed and open to outsourcing the overflow).
   - LinkedIn Jobs: filter `South Africa` + `Software Engineer / Product Manager / CTO` posted in last 14 days. A mid-market company hiring its first or second engineer is a prime outsourcing prospect.
   - Pnet, CareerJunction, Careers24 with `developer` + non-tech-vertical employers.
2. **Funding events** (capital + roadmap pressure):
   - Disrupt Africa, Ventureburn, Daily Maverick Business, Tech in Africa, Africa: The Big Deal newsletter.
   - SA VCs to monitor: Knife Capital, 4DI Capital, Newtown Partners, Kalon Venture Partners, Hlayisani Capital, E4E Africa, Naspers Foundry, AlphaCode (RMB).
3. **Public RFPs / tender boards:**
   - National Treasury eTenders portal (filter `software`, `system`, `application`, `digital`).
   - Provincial gov, SOEs (treat carefully — long sales cycles).
   - University & municipal RFPs (UCT, Wits, Stellenbosch, CoCT, CoJ all run dev tenders).
4. **Digital-maturity gap signals:**
   - Mid-market SA companies with no app, a WordPress brochure site, but visible operational complexity (manual ops on social media, customer comms via WhatsApp Business at scale, PDF forms).
   - Companies that just hired a "Head of Digital", "CTO", or "Chief Innovation Officer" (LinkedIn role-change alerts).
5. **Industry events as lead lists:**
   - AfricaCom, AI Expo Africa, Finovate Africa, Insurtech Insights Cape Town, Seamless Africa, AgriTech Africa, Mining Indaba (vendor + delegate lists).
6. **Accelerator cohorts (post-graduation companies need to scale beyond MVP):**
   - Founders Factory Africa, Startupbootcamp AfriTech, Grindstone Accelerator, Endeavor SA, Aurik, Edge Growth.

Use these channels weekly to refresh the prospect file.

## PART 4 — RECOMMENDED NEXT ACTIONS

1. **Cut the Part 1 dev-shop list** against your own positioning. Pick 5 you most resemble and 5 you want to displace; analyse their public case studies, pricing, and sector logos.
2. **Take the Part 3 list and qualify in two passes:**
   - Pass A (1 hour): for each company, check LinkedIn for tech roles posted in the last 30 days. Companies with 1–3 open dev/product roles = warm. >5 roles = they're scaling and may welcome a pod.
   - Pass B: cross-check news + funding (Disrupt Africa, Daily Investor) for the last 6 months.
3. **Build a weekly intake** using the Part 2 signal sources. Aim for 20 net-new qualified prospects per week.
4. **Lead-magnet idea** suited to the gap: a free POPIA-compliance audit + a 30-min "AI workflow automation discovery" for SA SMEs — both are under-served by Tier 2 competitors.
